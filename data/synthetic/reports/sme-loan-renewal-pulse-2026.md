# SME Loan Renewal Pulse 2026

Commissioned by: SME Banking
Access: restricted
Method: Quantitative phone survey
Sample: n=450 SME borrowers due for renewal
Fieldwork: 12 May 2026 to 5 June 2026
Coverage: Calabarzon and Central Luzon; owners aged 25-55
Segment: SME borrowers

Synthetic report for the demo. Not real BPI data.

## Summary

A short pulse among SME borrowers whose term loans were due for renewal, asking how they would
prefer to renew and what slowed down their last renewal.

## Key findings

- 61% of borrowers said they would renew online if their documents were already on file (base n=450).
- Among borrowers aged 25-34, 72% preferred to renew online (base n=140).
- Re-submitting documents the bank already holds was the most common complaint about the last renewal (rank 1 of 5).
- Waiting for a call back from the branch was cited by 34% of borrowers (base n=450).
