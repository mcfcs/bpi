# Cebu SME Digital Onboarding Focus Groups 2026

Commissioned by: Strategy & Insights
Access: open
Method: Qualitative focus groups (4 FGDs)
Sample: 4 focus groups, 28 participants
Fieldwork: 2 to 20 August 2026
Coverage: Cebu; owners aged 25-50
Segment: Micro and small business owners

Synthetic report for the demo. Not real BPI data.

## Summary

Four focus groups with micro and small business owners in Cebu on opening a business account and
applying for credit through the mobile app.

## Key findings

- Participants described the business account sign-up as clear until the document upload step.
- Several groups said a named contact person would make them more willing to finish an online application.
- Participants described fees as acceptable when they are shown before the application starts.

## In their words

> "I finished the personal account in ten minutes, but the business one asked for papers I don't keep on my phone." — Print shop owner, 33, Cebu City
> "Show me the fees at the start and I'm fine. Just don't surprise me at the end." — Food stall owner, 44, Mandaue
> "If the branch had just called me back on 0917 555 0198, I would have finished it." — Hardware store owner, 51, Lapu-Lapu
