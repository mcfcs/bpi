# Pitch deck: Evidence Brief (BPI DataWave 2026)

For the 13 Nov submission: pitch deck plus a recorded presentation of 10–15 minutes.

- `Evidence-Brief-Pitch-Deck.pdf`: the deck, 17 slides at 1920×1080. The box at the top right of
  each slide names the required section it covers (A customer problem, B proposed solution,
  C role of AI, D expected benefits and impact, E feasibility and implementation).
- `script.md`: what to say on each slide, with running times (about 13–14¼ minutes) and a click-by-click
  live demo on slide 6.
- `slides.html`: the source. Look after the team's Bantay-Baha deck (Bebas Neue, Barlow, Space Mono),
  in a light BPI palette (warm white, red highlights, gold tags). `img/` holds prototype screenshots (synthetic data).
- `previous/`: the earlier deck organised by the judging score sheet.

To re-export after editing: open `slides.html` in Edge or Chrome, Print, "Save as PDF", margins
None, "Background graphics" on. Fill in `[Team name]` on slides 1 and 16 first.

Figures come from `docs/HANDOFF.md` and the `backend/eval` runs. Token use and cost were measured
on 6 Bedrock briefs (Claude Sonnet 5.5, medium effort, 16 Nov 2026). Pilot targets and hosting
costs are proposals and estimates.
