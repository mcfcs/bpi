# Pitch deck: S&I Evidence Assistant (BPI DataWave 2026)

- `DataWave-2026-Evidence-Assistant.pdf`: the deck, 17 slides at 1920×1080.
- `slides.html`: the source. Each `<section>` is one slide; `img/` holds prototype screenshots (synthetic data).

Each slide's small red label names the judging criterion it answers.

To re-export after editing: open `slides.html` in Edge or Chrome, Print, choose "Save as PDF",
set margins to None and turn on "Background graphics". Placeholders to fill: `[Team name]`,
`[Members]`, `[Contact]`.

Figures come from `docs/HANDOFF.md` and the `backend/eval` runs (token use and cost: 6 Bedrock
briefs, Claude Sonnet 5.5 at medium effort, 16 Nov 2026). Pilot targets and infrastructure costs are
proposals and estimates, and the slides say so.
